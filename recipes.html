Healthy Recipes for Every Need

Science-backed recipes designed for diabetes, heart health, boosting blood levels, weight management and more — using ingredients easily found in Kenya.

🩸 Diabetes-Friendly Recipes

Low glycemic index meals that help keep blood sugar stable.

🥗

Sukuma Wiki & Egg Power Bowl

⏱ 20 min · 🔥 290 kcal · GI: Low

Steamed sukuma wiki sautéed with onions, served with boiled eggs and a small portion of brown ugali.

Low GI High Protein Budget
Benefits: Stabilizes blood sugar, rich in fibre and protein.
🍲

Lentil & Vegetable Stew

⏱ 35 min · 🔥 310 kcal · GI: Low

Green lentils simmered with carrots, tomatoes, spinach and mild spices. Serve with a small portion of sweet potato.

Low GI High Fibre Plant Protein
Benefits: Slow-release energy, excellent for blood sugar control.
🐟

Grilled Tilapia with Greens

⏱ 25 min · 🔥 280 kcal · GI: Very Low

Fresh tilapia seasoned with garlic, lemon and herbs, grilled and served with steamed terere or managu.

Low Carb Omega-3 Heart Safe
Benefits: Supports healthy blood sugar and heart function.

❤️ Heart-Healthy Recipes

Recipes low in saturated fat and rich in heart-protective nutrients.

🥑

Avocado & Bean Salad

⏱ 15 min · 🔥 320 kcal

Ripe avocado mixed with boiled kidney beans, tomatoes, red onion, cucumber and lemon-olive oil dressing.

Heart Healthy Healthy Fats Fibre
Benefits: Lowers bad cholesterol, supports healthy blood pressure.
🥣

Oats Porridge with Groundnuts

⏱ 12 min · 🔥 300 kcal

Rolled oats cooked in water or low-fat milk, topped with crushed groundnuts and a slice of banana.

Heart Healthy Soluble Fibre Breakfast
Benefits: Helps reduce cholesterol and keeps you full longer.
🥬

Steamed Fish with Spinach

⏱ 30 min · 🔥 270 kcal

Steamed white fish fillet with garlic, ginger and plenty of spinach or kunde. Light and nourishing.

Low Fat Omega-3 Potassium
Benefits: Supports healthy blood pressure and heart rhythm.

Beef or chicken liver quickly fried with onions, tomatoes and fresh spinach. High in heme iron.

High Iron Vitamin A Protein
Benefits: Excellent for raising haemoglobin levels quickly.
🫘

Bean & Sukuma Mash

⏱ 30 min · 🔥 310 kcal

Boiled beans mashed with sautéed sukuma wiki, onions and a squeeze of lemon (vitamin C helps iron absorption).

Plant Iron Fibre Budget
Benefits: Affordable iron source + vitamin C for better absorption.
🥜

Millet Porridge with Groundnuts

⏱ 20 min · 🔥 330 kcal

Traditional millet (wimbi) porridge enriched with groundnut paste and a little milk.

Iron Energy Traditional
Benefits: Supports blood building and sustained energy.
